About Royal Frame Studios

Founded in Jaipur, Royal Frame Studios specializes in documenting the most intimate and grand moments of Rajasthan's luxury weddings. We blend candid storytelling with royal aesthetics — capturing the chaos, the calm, and the love in between.

This website is a live academic project developed by a team of 5 B.Tech students to demonstrate advanced SEO, technical deployment, and content strategy.

Our Philosophy

We believe a wedding isn't a photoshoot — it's a once-in-a-lifetime story. Our approach is discreet, emotional, and cinematic. From the bride's haldi at a heritage haveli to the last dance at Rambagh Palace, we document every layer of your celebration.
